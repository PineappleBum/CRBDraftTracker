Average Finish uses a normalised formula that accounts for different league sizes: avg((rank−1)/(field−1)) × (global avg field − 1) + 1. Medals count the best three monthly scores in every season. GW highs/lows only cover seasons with gameweek-by-gameweek scores (20/21 onwards).
